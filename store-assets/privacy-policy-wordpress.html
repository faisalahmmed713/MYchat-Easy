MYchat Easy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

The short version

To use MYchat Easy you sign in with Google, and we keep your Google email and name so we know who uses it and can read and answer your feedback. We never see your password, the text you translate or write, or your API keys: your text goes only to the AI provider you choose, only when you ask it to.

Who we are

MYchat Easy is a browser extension published by Digital Aid IT ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the extension handles and how. If you have questions, contact us at support@digitalaidit.com.

Information we collect

MYchat Easy requires you to sign in with your Google account. When you sign in, and once a day while you use the extension, we receive and store:

  • Your Google account email address and name
  • The date you first signed in and the date you last used the extension
  • The extension version and your browser type

If you send feedback from the extension, we also store your rating and message together with your email and name.

Sign-in is handled by Google. We never see or store your Google password. We do not receive the text you translate, rewrite or write, the pages you visit, or your AI provider API keys. MYchat Easy does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use and store this information

We use your email and name only to give you access to MYchat Easy, to understand how many people use it and which versions are in use, and to read and reply to your feedback. This information is stored in a private Google Sheet owned by Digital Aid IT, which only Digital Aid IT can access. We do not sell, rent or share it with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

To see or delete the information we hold about you, email us from the address you signed in with and we will reply within 30 days. Signing out of the extension stops all further updates to your record.

Information stored on your device

The extension saves the following in your browser's local extension storage so it can work. This data never leaves your device unless stated below:

  • API keys you enter for AI providers
  • Your signed-in Google email, name and profile picture, so the extension can show who is signed in
  • Your settings, such as chosen provider and model, languages, tone and feature switches
  • Templates you create
  • History of your recent results (up to 50 items). You can turn history off or clear it at any time in the extension's settings.
  • Usage counts (number of requests and tokens) used to show estimated costs

Removing the extension deletes all of this data.

Information sent to AI providers

MYchat Easy works with an AI provider that you choose and connect with your own API key: Google Gemini, Groq, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, or another compatible service you set up (such as OpenRouter). When you use a feature, the extension sends the following directly from your browser to that provider:

  • The text you choose to translate, rewrite or write from (the text in the box, or the part you selected)
  • Text you select on a page and ask to translate or get reply ideas for
  • Your voice recording, only if you use voice input with the AI speech engine

Nothing is sent until you click a MYchat Easy button or use one of its keyboard shortcuts. The extension does not read or send the content of pages on its own.

Your use of each provider is governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy. Some free tiers, such as Google's free Gemini API tier, may use submitted content to improve their services, so avoid sending confidential information through a free tier. Please review the policies of the provider you choose:

  • Google Gemini API terms
  • Groq privacy policy
  • Anthropic privacy policy
  • OpenAI privacy policy
  • OpenRouter privacy policy

Microphone and voice

The extension only uses your microphone when you start voice input, and your browser asks for your permission first. With the Browser speech engine, speech recognition is provided by your browser (in Google Chrome, audio may be processed by Google's speech service). With the AI speech engine, the recording is sent to the AI provider you chose, as described above. Recordings are not stored by MYchat Easy.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses the text-to-speech voices built into your browser and operating system.

How we use information

Data handled by MYchat Easy is used only to provide the features you request. We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties (other than the AI provider you choose, to perform the actions you request), use it for advertising, use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes, or use it for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose.

The use of information received by MYchat Easy adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • Identity: to let you sign in with your Google account.
  • Storage: to save your settings, keys, templates and history on your device.
  • Access to AI provider websites: to send your requests to the provider you choose.
  • Running on web pages: to show the MYchat Easy button next to text boxes and the toolbar on text you select.
  • Active tab: to show whether the extension is turned on for the site you are viewing.
  • Optional site access: requested only if you connect a custom API, for that API's address only.

Security

Requests go directly from your browser to your chosen AI provider over encrypted HTTPS connections. Sign-in tokens are checked with Google before anything is written to our sheet. API keys are stored in your browser's extension storage and are only sent to the provider they belong to. Treat your API keys like passwords and consider setting spending limits in your provider's dashboard.

Children

MYchat Easy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and post the new version on this page.
